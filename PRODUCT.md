## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Review FTC match footage for up to four robots at once. Separate shots, successful Hive entries, and final pieces. Check Nectar pickup color and tally reviewed penalty points. Give each call a timestamp and a way to replay its evidence.

## Operating Context

Robo Scout is a local app for Robo Knights. It runs on Apple Silicon and keeps footage, calibration, and reviews on the Mac. BIOBUZZ TU08 is the first ruleset.
